# Sakura Garden

An interactive, brick-built cherry blossom garden framing a working Google search form.

**[Try the live demo](https://ayushiux.github.io/google-sakura-garden/)**

Click **Let it bloom**, switch on **A little breeze**, or drag the garden to look around. The pause button stops ambient motion. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Search submits directly to Google.

Open index.html directly in a modern browser; rendering requires WebGL and all assets are included.

An unofficial design study by Ayushi. Not affiliated with Google or LEGO. Google branding belongs to Google. Garden geometry is original; no official LEGO assets are used. Includes Three.js 0.169.0 under the MIT license (see THREE-LICENSE.txt).
